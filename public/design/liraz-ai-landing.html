Do I need to know how to code?+

No. Liraz AI is built for people with no programming background — you describe what you want in everyday language and the AI handles the technical work.

Websites, landing pages, simple web apps, booking tools, online stores, and internal tools — anything you can describe in a sentence or two is a good starting point.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes. Paid plans let you connect a custom domain, or you can publish instantly to a free liraz.ai subdomain.
